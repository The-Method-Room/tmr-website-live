  • Arrive 5–10 mins early. Doors close at class start time, and late arrivals are treated as a no-show

  • Cancel at least 24 hours before class to keep your class credit
  • Wipe down your reformer bed after class

Reformer Pilates FAQs

Find answers to your most common questions about our women's only reformer Pilates studio.

Do you offer beginner reformer Pilates classes?›

Yes — our Reformer Foundations class is designed specifically for beginners. If you're already comfortable with the basics, Reformer Flow is also a great next step. You'll learn alignment, breath and core control at your own pace in a supportive women's only environment.

Where is The Method Room located?›

We are at 1050–1052 London Road, Thornton Heath CR7 7ND, South London. Easily accessible by public transport with free parking nearby.

Is The Method Room a women's only studio?›

Yes, The Method Room is a women's only reformer Pilates studio. Our space is designed to be welcoming, inclusive and free from distraction.

What services do you offer?›

We offer a variety of Reformer Pilates classes: Foundations (beginner), Flow (intermediate), Strength (advanced), and Private sessions tailored to your body and goals.

How do I book?›

Booking classes is easy - choose your class, date and time in our schedule and secure your spot online. For merchandise, use the Shop page and you'll be taken to our Mindbody online store to complete your purchase. We recommend booking classes in advance to secure your preferred time slot.

What is your cancellation policy?›

We require a 24-hour notice for cancellations to avoid a cancellation fee. If you need to reschedule, please contact us as soon as possible.

What should I wear?›

Wear comfortable, flexible clothing. Grip socks are recommended to prevent slipping on the reformer. All classes are designed with focus on breath and precision.

Is Reformer Pilates suitable for beginners?›

Absolutely! Our Reformer Foundations class is designed specifically for beginners. If you are already comfortable with the basics, Reformer Flow is also a great option for building confidence and progressing your practice. The reformer provides support and resistance, making it accessible for all fitness levels. Our highly qualified instructors will guide you through proper form and progression.

Can I do Reformer Pilates if I have an injury?›

Yes. Reformer Pilates can be adapted for injury recovery and rehabilitation. Please mention any injuries during booking so we can modify movements and provide appropriate modifications. We recommend consulting your physiotherapist beforehand.

How long before I see results?›

Most clients notice improved posture, core strength, and muscle tone within 4–6 weeks of consistent practice (2–3 classes per week). Results depend on your starting fitness level, consistency, and individual goals.

Do I need prior Pilates experience?›

No prior experience necessary. Every class is designed for mixed abilities. Our instructors provide modifications for beginners and progressions for advanced practitioners, so everyone can work at their own level.

What's the difference between Drop-in and Class Packs?›

Drop-ins are one-off classes with no commitment. Class Packs offer better short-term value if you want flexibility. Memberships are designed for consistent weekly progress and include the best long-term value per class, depending on your tier. Check our Pricing section for current options and terms.

Can I freeze or transfer my class pack?›

Contact us directly at themethodroom@gmail.com or WhatsApp us to discuss your options. We'll advise based on your plan type (drop-in, class pack or membership) and support with flexibility where possible.

Do you offer private sessions?›

Yes! We offer Private Reformer sessions (1:1) and Duo Private sessions (2:1). Private sessions are fully customized to your body, goals, and fitness level. Email us at themethodroom@gmail.com or WhatsApp us to enquire, or check our Pricing page for rates.
