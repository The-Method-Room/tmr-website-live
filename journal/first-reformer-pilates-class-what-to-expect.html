Your First Reformer Pilates Class: What to Expect, What to Wear and How to Prepare

September 11, 2026 4 min read By The Method Room

In short: Book a beginner class, wear fitted, comfortable clothing and grip socks, and arrive 5 to 10 minutes early. Tell your instructor about any injuries or pregnancy. Expect around 50 minutes of guided, supported work, and do not worry about being flexible or strong. Studios start on time, so doors may close at the class start.

September has a fresh-start feeling, and a lot of people book their first reformer Pilates class around now. If you are feeling a little nervous, that is completely normal. Here is a clear picture of what to expect, so that you can walk in feeling prepared.

Choosing your first class

Start with a class designed for beginners. At The Method Room, Reformer Foundations teaches everything from reformer setup to breathing and core engagement, and every class is capped at eight reformers so your instructor can give you real attention. There is nothing to prove, and no prior Pilates experience is needed.

What to wear

  • Fitted, comfortable clothing: leggings or fitted shorts and a close-fitting top allow your instructor to see your alignment. Avoid anything loose that can catch on the springs.
  • Grip socks: they keep your feet secure on the footbar and carriage. If you do not have a pair yet, you can pick up TMR grip socks from our shop.
  • No heavy jewellery: remove anything that could snag or scratch the equipment.

Before you arrive

  • Eat lightly: have a small snack an hour or two before, rather than a heavy meal.
  • Tell us about your body: mention any injuries, conditions or pregnancy when you book, so your instructor can adapt the class.
  • Arrive 5 to 10 minutes early: it gives you time to settle in, meet your instructor and get your reformer set up. Classes start on time, and once class begins, doors close, so please do not rely on a late entry.
  • Leave the extras at home: we have water and fresh fruit waiting for you at the studio.

What happens in the class

Your instructor will explain how the reformer works, including the footbar, headrest and spring settings, and help you get set up. Over around 50 minutes you will move through exercises lying, kneeling, sitting and standing, with plenty of cues to explain what you are doing and what you should feel. Your instructor will offer options if something does not feel right for you, so you can always work at your own level.

What it feels like

Expect to feel your core, glutes and legs working, sometimes in places you did not know you had. You might feel a little wobbly. That is normal, and it is a good sign that you are working on stability. It should feel challenging, not painful. If something hurts, tell your instructor straight away.

Studio etiquette in brief

To keep the studio calm for everyone: arrive early, keep phones off and out of sight, wipe down your reformer after class, and avoid photography or filming without permission. Pre-booking is required, and our 24-hour cancellation policy applies. You can read the full details in our Terms of Service.

After your class

You may notice some muscle tiredness in the next day or two, particularly in your core and legs. Drink water, walk a little and carry on with your day. Most people feel more body awareness after their first few sessions, and strength and posture changes build over four to six weeks with two to three classes a week.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be flexible or strong to start reformer Pilates?

No. The springs and carriage support you, and your instructor will adapt each exercise. Flexibility and strength build with practice.

What if I arrive late?

Classes start on time and, for safety and to avoid disrupting others, doors close at the start of class. Late arrivals who cannot be admitted are treated as a no-show, so please aim to arrive 5 to 10 minutes early.

What should I tell my instructor?

Mention any injuries, medical conditions or pregnancy when you book and again before class, so your instructor can modify exercises.

How many classes should I try first?

Our intro offer includes two classes within two weeks. Many people then settle into one to three classes per week, depending on their goals.
